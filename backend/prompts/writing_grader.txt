### Evaluation Checklist (Bands 5–9)

#### **Task Achievement / Task Response**
* **Band 9:** Requirements fully satisfied; message followed effortlessly; rare lapses in content.
* **Band 8:** Requirements sufficiently covered; key features skillfully selected and illustrated.
* **Band 7:** Clear overview/purpose; relevant and accurate; format is appropriate.
* **Band 6:** Focuses on task; relevant overview attempted; may have some irrelevant or missing detail.
* **Band 5:** Addresses task but format may be inappropriate; detail is mechanical; lacks a "big picture" overview; includes irrelevant or inaccurate material.

#### **Coherence & Cohesion**
* **Band 9:** Seamless cohesion; skillful paragraphing; message followed without effort.
* **Band 8:** Logically sequenced; well-managed cohesion; occasional minor lapses.
* **Band 7:** Clear progression throughout; variety of cohesive devices used (though some inaccuracies).
* **Band 6:** Generally coherent with clear progression; cohesive devices may be mechanical or faulty.
* **Band 5:** Organisation is evident but not wholly logical; sentences are not fluently linked; repetitive due to inadequate use of reference/substitution.

#### **Lexical Resource (Vocabulary)**
* **Band 9:** Sophisticated and natural use of lexical features; errors are extremely rare.
* **Band 8:** Fluently used to convey precise meanings; skillful use of uncommon/idiomatic items.
* **Band 7:** Sufficient flexibility and precision; awareness of style and collocation is evident.
* **Band 6:** Meaning is clear; restricted range or lack of precision; errors do not impede communication.
* **Band 5:** Limited/simple vocabulary; frequent lapses in word choice; spelling/formation errors may cause difficulty for the reader.

#### **Grammatical Range & Accuracy**
* **Band 9:** Full flexibility and control; punctuation and grammar are used appropriately throughout.
* **Band 8:** Wide range of structures used accurately; majority of sentences are error-free.
* **Band 7:** Variety of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences; grammar well-controlled.
* **Band 6:** Mix of simple and complex forms; limited flexibility; errors rarely impede communication.
* **Band 5:** Range is limited and repetitive; complex sentences are attempted but tend to be faulty; errors cause difficulty for the reader.
